Pricing

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The current ReviewRouter CI control plane is free. Optional hosted API reviews are coming soon and unavailable for purchase.

Proposed prelaunch pricing. These numbers and rules are a draft, not a binding offer. No checkout is available. Final prices, model rates, taxes, expiry, and purchase conditions will be disclosed before any sale.

Free today: your CI, your provider

Use ReviewRouter to configure and manage reviews that execute in your authorized CI. Your selected provider subscriptions or API charges and CI or runner costs remain your responsibility under your own arrangements. You do not need a proposed paid plan to keep using this free control plane.

Proposed monthly hosted API plans

For the proposed paid option, ReviewRouter's backend would process authorized review context and call provider APIs. The operator would pay those provider API costs. The plans below would provide model-metered usage rather than a fixed number of reviews.

Proposed usage and balance rules

  • Same usage value: 1 monthly unit = 1 purchased credit for usage deduction. The same model-specific deduction rates would apply to both balances.
  • Plan first: usage would consume monthly units first. Purchased credits would only be used after the user explicitly opts in to using that balance.
  • Monthly reset: the proposed monthly allowance would reset at renewal with no rollover of unused monthly units.
  • Separate purchased balance: purchased credits would not reset at monthly renewal. Their expiry and whether an active subscription is required to use them are provisional and will be disclosed before purchase.
  • Model metering: model-specific rates and deduction details remain provisional. We will show the applicable rates before purchase and paid use. A review's actual consumption varies with its context and execution.
